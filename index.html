RESEARCH

From visual perception to future-state generation

World models for interactive environments

Using diffusion models and flow matching, I study how to unify perception and generation through long-video synthesis, motion generation and editing, depth estimation, and self-supervised representation learning. My work also explores efficient architectures, sparse computation, and iterative refinement at inference time, alongside closed-loop mechanisms that combine state tracking, decisions, and feedback.

Efficient inference & interaction
