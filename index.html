World models for interactive, closed-loop simulation.

I am a Full Professor at HUST and lead VIBE Lab. My research centers on world models: learning geometric and semantic representations, modeling how environments evolve, and generating controllable future states.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Amsterdam, advised by Cees G. M. Snoek and Pascal Mettes, and was a postdoctoral researcher in Björn Ommer’s lab at LMU Munich.
